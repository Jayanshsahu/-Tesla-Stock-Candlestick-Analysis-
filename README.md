📊 -Tesla-Stock-Candlestick-Analysis-

                 📈 A 4-year Power BI analysis of Tesla's daily stock data (2021–2024), featuring candlestick patterns,
                support/resistance levels, financial metrics, and key market events to uncover trends and future outlooks
                for TSLA investors.
                #PowerBI #CandlestickAnalysis #TeslaStock #FinancialAnalysis #DAX #TechnicalAnalysis #StockMarketInsights #TSLA


🧠 Problem Statement
                Tesla (TSLA) has been one of the most volatile and widely discussed stocks of the decade.
                Between 2021 and 2024, it saw unprecedented highs and significant downturns due to macroeconomic,
                operational, and leadership-driven changes.

                The goal of this project is to analyze Tesla’s daily candlestick data over 4 years to understand:

                Stock price movements

                Volatility patterns

                Support/resistance levels

                Market sentiment triggers

                This analysis aims to deliver actionable insights for traders, investors, and financial
                analysts using Power BI and advanced data visualization techniques.

🔍 Key Insights



            Volatility Triggered by Macroeconomics: Rate hikes and inflation in 2022 caused tech sell-offs.

            Leadership Distraction: Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter led to investor concerns.

            Margin Compression: Aggressive price cuts to defend EV market share impacted profitability.

            Expansion Signals: New gigafactories (Texas, Berlin) and growing energy segment (Powerwall, Megapack).

            ESG & Regulatory Risks: Removal from S&P ESG Index in 2022 raised governance issues
